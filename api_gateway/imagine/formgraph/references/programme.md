# Programme: phases, domains and records

The programme view implements the hardware product design guide: primitives across disciplines, all twelve domain groups, the phase sequence with gates and loops, the effort matrix and the V-model. The registry is `data/programme.json`; the builder embeds it in every page.

## Phases and exit criteria

Define, Concept, EVT, DVT, PVT, Mass production, In service / end of life. Failing a later phase sends the product back (EVT to Concept, DVT to EVT, PVT to DVT); the page records each loop with its reason.

| Phase | Exit criteria the page checks |
|---|---|
| Define | intent ledger reviewed, assumptions confirmed, markets chosen, every requirement has a value, every requirement has a planned test |
| Concept | gate 2 (no blocking problems), gate 3 (every Must met), interfaces drafted, trade-offs have reasons, top risks have mitigations, compliance plan, budgets inside targets |
| EVT | gate 4 (background validation), EVT tests passed, interfaces frozen, production tooling ordered (moulding) |
| DVT | DVT tests passed, certification passed, BOM released and unchanged, technical file complete |
| PVT | PVT tests passed, long-lead parts second-sourced, measured yield at or above target |
| Production | field quality tracking in place, change control active |
| In service | repair and take-back in place, end-of-life route recorded |

## What is computed and what is tracked

Computed in the page, with a pass, at-risk or fail status:

- **Budgets:** mass, average power, BOM cost, heat load against the touch limit, and the temperature error budget combined by root sum of squares.
- **Tolerance:** lid-to-shell fit stack-up by Monte Carlo (20,000 samples) with Cpk.
- **Vibration:** first panel mode, clamped plate with rib stiffening.
- **Reliability:** MTBF by parts count with an Arrhenius temperature factor, and survival over the target life; derating of every rail, cell and wire.
- **Electronics:**
  - junction temperatures per part
  - PCB trace widths by IPC-2221
  - creepage and clearance by voltage class
  - USB impedance constraint
- **Assembly:** DFA part count, assembly time and design efficiency.
- **Packaging and logistics:** carton size, packed mass, volumetric and chargeable weight.
- **Sustainability:** cradle-to-gate carbon, repair score, recyclability and material marking.
- **Business:** unit economics and margin.
- **Schedule:** phase dates, with long-lead order-by dates for tooling, test slots, cells and PCBs.
- **Supply:** lead times and single-source risk.
- **Industrial design and human factors:**
  - form scores: proportion, stance, edge language
  - human factors: grip span, one-hand mass, button size and force, sightlines, edges and pinch points
  - feedback coverage per event
- **Security:** checklist, including UK PSTI where it applies.
- **Compliance:** applicable standards from markets and functions.
- **Test plan:** generated by phase and V-model level, with traceability from every requirement to a test.
- **Risk:** FMEA with occurrence driven by live checks.
- **Interfaces:** mechanical, electrical, data, thermal, RF and user interfaces from the design.
- **Change control:** ECO draft against the last release.

Tracked with an owner, status and evidence, never shown as passing on its own:

- user research and usability testing
- mechanisms and multibody dynamics, control systems
- cloud, companion apps, data and analytics
- electronics manufacturing detail, root cause analysis, field quality
- industry-specific and functional safety
- intellectual property, contracts, liability insurance
- layout-level PCB rules (DRC)
- full FEA, CFD, SPICE and EM solver runs, which are estimated in the validation pack

## Environment

The `environment` block drives these checks, shown under "Where it lives" and fed into the BOM, the gates, the FMEA, the interfaces and the test plan:
- charging on the hottest day, with sun on the body colour
- charging below 0 °C
- electronics on the hottest day
- battery life and brownout at the coldest temperature
- the material's cold and hot range, UV, salt corrosion of finish and fixings, chemicals
- condensation (vent membrane) and board coating
- altitude (cooling and clearance factors)
- vibration against the panel's first mode, and connector retention
- battery storage temperature

The test plan picks up IEC 60068 conditions from it:
- cold (-2-1), dry heat (-2-2), change of temperature (-2-14)
- damp heat (-2-78 steady, -2-30 cyclic), salt mist (-2-52)
- vibration (-2-6 or -2-64 by profile), shock (-2-27), solar radiation (-2-5), low pressure (-2-13)
- UV per ISO 4892, ingress per IEC 60529, drop per IEC 60068-2-31, repeated cold when it operates below 0 °C

## Domain status

Each domain shows Pass, At risk, Fail, Open, Planned or n/a:

- **Modelled:** the status comes from its hooks.
- **Partly modelled:** combines its hooks with the owner's status.
- **Tracked:** stays Open until the owner marks it done and attaches evidence.
- **n/a:** the domain does not apply to this product, for example optics without a light, or battery systems without a cell.

## What to put in the config

Everything under `programme` in the product config:

- `markets`, `segment`, `sector`, `environment`, `start`
- `ledger` (every statement of the brief), `assumptions`, `customRequirements`
- `rationale` (process, material, board, battery, radio, size), `tradeoffs`, `risks`, `interfaces`
- `human`, `software`, `firmware` (security fields), `budgets`, `business`, `lifecycle`, `sustainability`, `packaging`, `production`, `supply`, `flows`
