# Getting around

Every page except the 3D twin shares the same navigation and top bar. Learn this once and it applies everywhere.

## Navigation rail (left side)

Your main menu, in two groups:

- **Workspace:** Projects, Agent sessions, Runs, Approvals.
- **Engineering:** Digital twin, Bill of materials, Files & artifacts, Knowledge, Compliance.

At the bottom you will find **Settings & connection** and a **Documentation** link. Hover any icon to see its name. You can expand the rail to show full labels, and on small screens it becomes a slide-in panel — tap outside it to close it.

## Top bar

- **Breadcrumbs** show where you are (for example *Projects › Details* when you open a single project).
- **Project picker** chooses which project the engineering pages show. If a list suddenly looks empty, check which project is selected here — parts, models, files, and compliance all follow this picker.
- **Appearance** switches between Light, Dark, and System. Your choice is remembered on this browser.
- **Sample** opens a built-in example workspace you can explore with no connection. While it is open, the same button reads **Exit sample**.
- **Account icon** takes you to Settings.
- **Connection indicator** shows whether the dashboard can reach your gateway: *Connecting*, *Connected*, or *Unavailable*. If a page is blank or shows dashes instead of numbers, glance here first — it usually means there is no connection, not that your data is gone.

## When the connection drops

If the gateway cannot be reached, pages show a **Connect your engineering gateway** message with two buttons:

- **Retry** — try the same connection again.
- **Set up connection** — go to Settings and check the address.

To connect: open **Settings**, enter the gateway address and port your administrator gave you (or leave both empty if you were told the dashboard connects on its own), choose **Test connection** to check it safely, then **Save**.
