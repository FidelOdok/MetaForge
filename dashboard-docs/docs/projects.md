# Projects

Projects keep everything together — design runs, 3D models, parts, and files all belong to a project.

## Project workspace

This is the dashboard home. Use it to create projects, find a project, and see what needs your attention.

**What you can do here:**

- **See the big picture** in the four cards at the top: active projects, runs in progress, items awaiting your review, and work products. Each card is a shortcut — select it to jump to that page.
- **Create a project** with **New project** (top right). It appears in your library straight away.
- **Find a project** with the search box, the *All · Active · Draft · Archived* filters, and sorting by recent updates or name. Switch between card grid and list views with the two icons.
- **Catch up on reviews** in the review queue (side panel): runs paused waiting for your decision. Select one to open it, or choose **Open approvals** for the full list.
- **See latest work** in recent artifacts: the newest items across your projects. Select one to open its project, or choose **Browse files** to see all files.

If the connection is down you will see a **Connect your engineering gateway** message instead of your projects, with **Retry** and **Set up connection** buttons. If you have no projects yet, you will see a welcome message with a **Create a project** button.

## Project details

Select a project to open its detail page. Opening it also makes it the active project, so the twin, parts list, and files pages will show this project's data.

**What you can do here:**

- **Move forward** with the three buttons: **Start design run** (begins a new run for this project), **Open twin & agent** (explore the 3D model and ask questions), **Bill of materials** (see parts and costs).
- **Check health at a glance:** how many work products exist, what share is marked valid, how many agent tasks exist, and how many risk alerts need attention.
- **Open any work product** by selecting its row — it opens in the digital twin.
- **Review recent changes** in the artifact updates panel (newest first).
- **Rename** with the pencil icon, or **delete** with the trash icon (you will be asked to confirm; the underlying design data is kept and simply detached from the project).

If a project cannot be loaded, you will see a *Project could not be loaded* message with **Try again** and a link to connection settings.
